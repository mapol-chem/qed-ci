Pilot results for H₂ and LiH at equilibrium and stretched geometries compare the QED-CAS electronic 1-RDM error with its cavity-free counterpart; only cases meeting the 10⁻⁶ Eh photon-basis threshold are plotted. Panel (a) shows parity and panel (b) the QED/cavity-free error ratio: |λ|=0.01: median 1.13 (range 0.996–4.57, n=30); |λ|=0.05: median 1 (range 0.498–1.82, n=21); |λ|=0.10: median 1.02 (range 0.367–1.87, n=12). 3 LiH geometry/coupling cases did not converge by 10 photon states and are retained as flagged rows in photon_convergence.csv.
